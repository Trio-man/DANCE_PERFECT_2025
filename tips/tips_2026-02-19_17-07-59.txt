DANCE PRACTICE TIPS
========================================

Use these tips to improve your next run. Each tip corresponds to a moment in your performance.

• Keep your left arm bent from 0:00 to 0:03.

• Keep your right arm bent from 0:00 to 0:04.

• Straighten your left knee a little at 0:00.

• Straighten your right leg from 0:00 to 0:04.

• Keep your arms higher at 0:00.

• Keep your arms higher at 0:04.

• Straighten your left leg from 0:00 to 0:01.

• Straighten your left leg from 0:03 to 0:04.

• Straighten your left leg at 0:02.

• Straighten your right knee a little at 0:02.

• Straighten your left arm at 0:04.

• Lean your torso forward from 0:00 to 0:04.

• Raise your left arm to match the reference from 0:00 to 0:04.

• Slow down to match the reference from 0:00 to 0:04.
