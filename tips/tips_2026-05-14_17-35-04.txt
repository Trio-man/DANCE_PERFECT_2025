DANCE PRACTICE TIPS
========================================

Use these tips to improve your next run. Each tip corresponds to a moment in your performance.

• Straighten your legs at 0:00.

• Straighten your left leg from 0:02 to 0:03.

• Straighten your legs at 0:05.

• Straighten your legs at 0:09.

• Straighten your right leg at 0:02.

• Keep your right arm bent at 0:03.

• Straighten your left arm at 0:05.

• Straighten your left knee a little at 0:07.

• Speed up to match the reference from 0:02 to 0:03.

• Lean your torso back from 0:02 to 0:03.

• Lean your torso back at 0:05.

• Lean your torso back at 0:07.

• Lean your torso back at 0:09.

• Raise your left arm to match the reference at 0:07.
